**Goal:** Build Hong's two answers of set anymap182 (2026-11-16): (1) the walker adds sideways teleport links in its own route step by his 4 px landing rule, so a gap whose two sides differ by 3 or 4 px in height is crossed, and the map editor's preview shows those links and its orphan check counts them; (2) a gap is offered only when an 18 px teleport crosses it from anywhere the planner counts as lined up, so gaps of 17 to 19 px get no crossing.

**Architecture:** Both rules live in `src/route/route_graph.py` (frozen; opened by Hong's answers, quoted per task). Rule 2 is one change in `RouteGraph.gap_launch` (the exact-A fallback goes; a launch is returned only when the shortest recorded teleport carries from the whole lined-up range). Rule 1 is a new step in `RouteGraph.build`: from every platform end that stops a teleport, `side_landing` says where the teleport lands, and when that is another platform with no edge to it yet, a `GAP` edge is added (priced by `gap_launch`, so rule 2 caps it too) and its ends join `split_ends`. The added links are kept in a new attribute `RouteGraph.teleport_links`, which the map editor reads to draw them. The planner, `route_preview.py` and the simulator need no change: the new links are ordinary `GAP` edges.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.14, networkx 3.6.1, returns, Tk (map editor), pytest; the route simulator (`src/route/sim/`).
