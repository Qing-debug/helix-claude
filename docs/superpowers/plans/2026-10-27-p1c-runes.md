## Design choices (spec C "Left to the builder", specC:L70-L75)

DC1. **Spawn signal = the rune icon on the minimap.** The minimap is already captured every frame for B, so a
masked template match on it (B15's `CharacterLocator` technique) costs nothing extra and gives the rune's minimap
position, which is the movement target. The icon template is cut from real frames (C12). If C12 finds no rune icon
on any frame in HX's data, C12 stops and returns: the spawn sample then needs passive capture ([Hong] H-2).

DC2. **Rune position → movement target.** `locate.attach(rg, rune_pos)` anchors the rune to a platform of the
`RouteGraph`; the target node is the anchor's nearest node by x on that platform; `walk_leg` takes the bot there
with B's planner; a final approach (`InputLayer.walk` toward the rune's x, using `MovementParams.walk_px_per_s`)
closes the remaining gap until `|char_x − rune_x| ≤ RuneParams.reach_px` on the same platform (measured y within
`RuneParams.same_platform_px`). A rune that does not attach (`Failure`) is logged `UNREACHED` and retried only when
the watch reports it at a new position. No B planner change.

DC3. **"Finish the current leg"** = the trip starts only between legs: after a leg's record is written with
outcome `REACHED` (C19 hook). A rune that appears mid-leg waits for the leg's end, however long (B's own
`leg_timeout_s` bounds the leg). Behind the bot or far away makes no difference: the trip goes after the leg ends
(specC:L12, L20). After the trip the walker continues with the next leg of the rotation from the measured position
(B's planner re-plans from where the character is).
**Rule for a timed-out leg:** a leg counts as finished when B's walker itself goes on to the next leg, so a leg that
ends `TIMED_OUT` also lets the trip run (the bot is somewhere on the map; the trip plans from the measured position);
`STOPPED` and `LINK_DOWN` never do. Evidence: `LegOutcome` has exactly these four values (`src/route/leg_log.py:31-35`),
and `_run_laps` returns only on `STOPPED` or `LINK_DOWN` and goes on after `TIMED_OUT` (`src/route/walker.py:250,262`).
The spec leaves the edges of "finish the current leg" to the builder (specC:L73), so this is a builder's choice, not a
decision for Hong; it errs toward the rune, the costlier miss (specC:L7).

DC4. **Solve outcome = the rune icon leaves the minimap.** Success: after the last arrow key, C17's outcome reader (reading the
minimap itself at frame rate, not through the 1 Hz watch; piece 3c) sees no rune
for `RuneParams.gone_frames` consecutive minimap frames within `RuneParams.outcome_window_s`. Failure: the panel is
gone from the screen and the rune is still on the minimap after that window. Anything else is `UNKNOWN` and treated
as failure for the retry. Both cases are checked on collected examples (specC:L63) in C17 and C22.

DC5. **Retry loop.** After any non-success attempt the trip waits 8 s (`RuneParams.retry_wait_s`, specC:L15) from the
attempt's end, re-checks that the rune is still on the minimap (if gone: `RUNE_GONE`, trip ends), re-approaches if
the character moved off reach, and tries again; it repeats until solved, gone, stopped or the link is down
(specC:L15; no attempt cap, specC:L21 puts channel changes in Phase 3).

DC6. **Reaction timing.** The arrow keys go out as one `InputLayer.sequence(keys, stimulus_ns=t_read_ns)`, where
`t_read_ns` is the `perf_counter_ns` of the frame in which the panel was read (for spinning arrows: the end of the
burst). Space is `InputLayer.tap(Key.SPACE, stimulus_ns=t_arrived_ns)`. Feature A draws all holds and gaps.

DC7. **Spinning method** is chosen in C11 from all three candidates of specC:L29-L33, built and compared side by side
(Hong, QB93: the pose model is C10b), on the clips C9 cuts from Hong's labelled gameplay videos (N3PC67-D3). How the
answers are used, how the method is chosen and what bar it must pass is the judging rule, one slot in C11 (step J;
N3PC67-D7, open: Hong picks). It ships with `ACCURACY_UNVERIFIED = True` until it passes that rule. The flag is a
module constant in `spinning.py` and is copied into every `RuneAttempt` record (specC:L34).

DC8. **Attempt log** is a new file `rune_attempts.jsonl` beside B's `legs.jsonl`, written like B's leg log (one
`write`, `flush`, `fsync` per record, C9 of plan B). It is Feature D's input for rune events.

**Class:** O — a matcher in B15's pattern plus a measurement tool whose result gates the spawn target. **Cloud:** no.

- [ ] **Matcher (`spawn.py`, R8).** The technique of `CharacterLocator` (`src/route/character.py:8-14, 24-30, 89-92`):
  greyscale, masked `cv2.TM_CCOEFF_NORMED`, non-finite scores and scores above `1 + 1e-3` scrubbed to −1. Copy the
  technique; do not import `character.py`'s private names and do not change `character.py`. Differences: no rune is
  the normal case (`Success(None)`, not a `Failure`); `Failure` is only for a bad frame (same checks and texts as
  `character.py:102-110`). `locate_all` picks matches strongest first: take the best score ≥ `min_score`, record it,
  scrub the square of side `2 · max(icon_w, icon_h) + 1` around it, repeat, at most `max_matches` (default 3). The
  position is the icon's centre `(left + w // 2, top + h // 2)`, as `character.py:99-100`. `locate` returns the first
  match's position or `None`. `from_files(icon=RUNE_ICON, mask=RUNE_MASK)` defaults to `assets/rune/` resolved from the
  package location and greys the icon with `cvtColor` (not `IMREAD_GRAYSCALE`, `character.py:75-77`). The constructor
  raises `ValueError` on arguments that cannot work, as `character.py:41-60`; `from_files` turns that into a `Failure`.
  `locate` and `locate_all` keep no mutable state: the watch and the trip call one locator from two threads (R8).
- [ ] **`MIN_SCORE`** is set by `tune` (below) and written into `spawn.py` as a constant with the tune summary in the
  commit body. Until then it is 0.9 (a placeholder the tests do not pin beyond `0 < MIN_SCORE <= 1`).
- [ ] **Sets.** `eval_spawn` reads one or more `--set DIR` (C12's layout: `index.csv`, `minimaps/`, `labels.json`, the
  Hong-checked file). A crop counts only if `native` is true and its label is not `null`; `[x, y]` is rune-present,
  `"absent"` is rune-free. Crops with identical pixels (sha256 of the array) count once, rune-present and rune-free
  alike, so a still recording cannot inflate either count (N4 B8). A **sighting** is one rune position on one map key:
  rune-present labels are grouped in id order, a label within 3 px (both axes) of a group's first label joining it.
  **Split:** a crop's map key is its `map` with a trailing ` x <n> - <language>` or `_YYYY-MM-DD` removed (so one
  map's English and Korean folders, and C21's dated capture folders, fall together; N4 B28); `tuning` if the first byte of
  `sha1(map key)` is even, else `heldout`. Rune-free crops are never used to choose anything, so all of them are
  scored.
- [ ] **`tune`** `... tune --set DIR [--set DIR ...]`: on tuning rune-present crops only, the score at the label (the
  best score whose match centre is within 2 px of the label on both axes). `MIN_SCORE = floor((min − 0.05) · 100) / 100`
  clamped to [0.5, 0.99]. Prints the value, the count it came from and the five lowest scores with ids. Writes nothing
  but its report.
- [ ] **`check`** `... check --set DIR [--set DIR ...] [--out DIR]`: runs `RuneLocator.from_files()` (the shipped
  `MIN_SCORE`) on held-out rune-present crops and all rune-free crops. Found = `locate` within 2 px of the label on
  both axes; wrong = found more than 3 px away in a straight line (a miss, listed); false = any position on a rune-free
  crop. **PASS** iff distinct held-out rune-present ≥ 100 from ≥ 20 held-out sightings on ≥ 3 map keys, with found
  ≥ 99%, and distinct rune-free ≥ 1,000 with false ≤ 0.1% (specC:L62; the counts are the least that make the two
  rates mean anything, and the sighting floor keeps one parked spawn from filling the present count, N4 B8). Below
  any count it prints `INSUFFICIENT` with what is missing. Reported beside the gate, not gating it but an input to DC4
  (N4 B1): the **overlap rate**, found on the crops where the character's dot is within one icon side of the rune,
  with its count (the dot can cover the icon there, never at spawn time, and C17 reads a covered icon as absent, so a
  low rate means false `SOLVED` reads); counts per set (each `--set` is one source), per map and per sighting; the
  ten highest rune-free scores with ids; mean `locate` time. Every `check` appends one line
  to `data/runes/spawn/heldout_looks.jsonl` (`{"stamp", "git_head", "min_score", "result", "counts"}`): a change to
  the method or `MIN_SCORE` after a look is a new look, and the report says how many there were. Output
  `data/runes/spawn/eval/<UTC stamp>/{report.json, summary.txt}`; refuses an existing `--out`.
- [ ] Exit codes: 0 PASS (`check`) or done (`tune`); 1 `FAIL` or `INSUFFICIENT`; 2 could not run: a missing `--set`,

### Task C14: Rune watch thread

**Files:** create `src/rune_solver/watch.py`; tests `tests/rune_solver/test_watch.py`,
`tests/rune_solver/rune_fakes.py` (shared fakes, flat-imported as `from rune_fakes import ...`, as
`tests/input/input_fakes.py`; C15 and C16 add to it).
**Class:** S — a daemon thread that reads a frame another thread writes, and a value read from a third thread.
**Cloud:** yes (numpy and cv2 only; the tests use a fake locator and a synthetic icon).
**Depends on:** C13 (`RuneLocator.locate_all`, `RuneMatch`). **Interface created:** R8's `RuneSighting` and
`RuneWatch` (amended by piece 3b: `looks_absent`, `absent_looks`, `moved_px`, `log`, `look`); used by C17, C18, C20.

- [ ] Read R8, DC1, DC3, Review Focus 3, and `src/route/position.py` (the frame-reading pattern this task copies:
  take the frame under `minimap_frame_lock`, compare it by identity with the last one, copy it, release the lock, then
  match on the copy, `position.py:30-39`). `shared` is duck-typed (`minimap_frame`, an RGB array or None, and
  `minimap_frame_lock`), so `watch.py` imports neither `src.main` nor `src.vision`; B's `SharedFrame`
  (`src/route/runtime.py:245-250`) is one such object.
- [ ] **`look()`** (one look; the thread calls it, tests call it directly):
  1. Under the lock take `shared.minimap_frame`. `None`, or the same object as the last frame looked at, is **not a
     look**: nothing changes and `latest()` is returned. (During a C16 burst the camera lock stops the minimap
     thread for about 1 s, D2, so the frame freezes; a frozen frame says nothing about the rune, so it must not count
     as "absent".) Otherwise copy it, remember the reference (not `id()`, as `position.py:27-28`), note
     `t = clock.now_ns()`, release the lock.
  2. `locator.locate_all(copy)` outside the lock. A `Failure` (a bad frame) is not a look either; it is logged once
     per distinct text (`rune watch: <text>`).
  3. `Success(())` is an **absent look**: if there is a current sighting, replace it with `present=False`,
     `looks_absent + 1`, everything else unchanged.
  4. `Success(matches)` is a **present look** at `matches[0].pos` (the strongest, R8). It starts a **new rune**
     (`rune + 1`, numbering from 1 per watch, `first_seen_ns = last_seen_ns = t`, `prev_look_ns` the stamp of the
     watch's previous look, absent or present, or `None` if this is its first; R8, N4 B19) when there is no current sighting,
     or the current one has `looks_absent >= absent_looks`, or the new position is more than `moved_px` from the
     current one on either axis (a rune does not move: a new place is a new rune, which is what lets C18 retry an
     `UNREACHED` rune only at a new position, DC2). Otherwise it updates the current rune: `pos` (the latest match),
     `last_seen_ns = t`, `present=True`, `looks_absent=0`.
  5. **Two or more matches** (Review Focus 3): the strongest is used as above and one line is logged on the first look
     of each run of multi-match looks (a look with ≤ 1 match ends the run): `rune watch: <n> matches
     (x, y) s=0.93, (x, y) s=0.71; using the strongest`.
  The current sighting is a frozen `RuneSighting` replaced whole under an internal `threading.Lock`; `latest()`
  returns it under that lock, so a reader never sees half an update.
- [ ] **Thread.** `start()` starts one daemon thread named `rune-watch` whose loop is: stop event set → return;
  `look()`; `stop_event.wait(period_s)` (wall time, so a stop is noticed at once; the stamps come from `clock`).
  An exception escaping `look()` (a locator bug) is caught in the loop, logged once per distinct text
  (`rune watch: look failed: <type>: <text>`), and the loop goes on: a dead watch would leave the trip reading an old
  sighting forever. `stop()` sets the event and joins with a 5 s timeout; it is idempotent and works before `start()`.
  A second `start()` raises `RuntimeError` (a programming error, as `threading.Thread.start`). The constructor raises
  `ValueError` for `period_s <= 0`, `absent_looks < 1` or `moved_px < 0`. `log=None` prints to stderr.
  `absent_looks` is a read-only property (R8; C18's gone rule reads it; N4 B13).
- [ ] **`rune_fakes.py`** (this task's part): `FakeShared` (a lock and a settable frame), `FakeLocator` (returns
  scripted `locate_all` results in order, records each call and whether `shared.minimap_frame_lock` was held during
  it), `FakeClock` (`now_ns` / `sleep` on one fake time, as `tests/input/input_fakes.py:20-32`).
- [ ] **Tests (`test_watch.py`)**, `look()` driven directly unless the test is about the thread:
  1. No frame → `latest()` is `None`, the locator is not called. The same frame object twice → one locator call.
  2. First present look at `(50, 40)` → `RuneSighting(rune=1, pos=(50, 40), first_seen_ns=t, last_seen_ns=t,
     present=True, looks_absent=0, prev_look_ns=None)`; a rune first seen after an absent look at `t0` has
     `prev_look_ns == t0`.
  3. Numbering, `absent_looks=3`: present, 2 absent, present → still rune 1 (`first_seen_ns` kept, `looks_absent`
     back to 0); present, 3 absent, present → rune 2 with a new `first_seen_ns`. The absent looks give `present=False`
     and `looks_absent` 1, 2, 3 with `pos` and `last_seen_ns` unchanged.
  4. Moved, `moved_px=4`: present at `(50, 40)` then `(54, 40)` → rune 1 at `(54, 40)`; then `(59, 40)` → rune 2.
  5. Frozen minimap: present, then 10 looks on the same frame object (as during a burst), then a new frame with the
     rune → still rune 1, `present=True` throughout, and the frozen looks never called the locator.
  6. Two matches → `pos` is `matches[0].pos`; the log gets one line for a run of 3 multi-match looks and a second
     line after a single-match look breaks the run.
  7. A locator `Failure` → not a look (`looks_absent` unchanged), one log line for 3 equal failures, two for two texts.
  8. The locator runs outside the frame lock (the fake records `locked()` as False on every call); a frame changed
     in place after the look does not change what was matched (the copy).
  9. Thread: `period_s=0.01`, a fake locator scripted over time; another thread calls `latest()` 10,000 times and

  10. A locator that raises on its second call → the thread logs `look failed` once and keeps looking (later calls

  11. Constructor arguments that cannot work → `ValueError`; `absent_looks` reads the constructor's value and cannot

  12. One real `RuneLocator` (built with its constructor on a synthetic icon and mask, C13 test pattern) on a

  13. Importing `src.rune_solver.watch` imports no `ultralytics`, `torch`, `bettercam` or `src.vision` (subprocess, as

**What a test this size can and cannot show** (exact binomial figures, `notes/D7R68.md`):
- It **can catch a bad reader.** With ~20 spinning arrows and a bar of at most 1 wrong, a reader that is right 80% of
  the time passes only 7% of the time; a 70% reader 1%.
- It **cannot tell a good reader from a very good one.** A reader right 90% of the time still passes that bar 39% of
  the time, and a truly 95% reader fails it 26% of the time. A pass at 19/20 only shows "at least about 78% per
  arrow" (one-sided 95% bound); 20/20 shows at least 86%. Showing 95% outright takes about 60 arrows in a row with
  none wrong.
- The two spinning arrows of one rune share a clip, a map and a spin, so they are not fully independent: the real
  sample is between 10 and 20, and every figure above is optimistic.
- Trials are only runes Hong's own answer solved (C11 step 2): a rune he failed, likely a harder one, is left out,
  so the score flatters the reader (N4 review A10).
- The pose reader trains on a split of Hong's clips (QB95), so it is scored only on the clips it did not train on:
  its count is smaller than the classical readers' unless he records more runes. The picked option says which clips
  are its train split (C10b, C11 step J).

  rate, or at another scale or codec, may count toward clearing the flag is decided together with this rule

  "if the recorder allows". Until then nothing is gated on it: C9 records each clip's `fps` and `frame_wh` and
  reports fps < 30 (C9's checks), and C11 step 1 leaves it pending.
- Why that may still be enough: the bot retries every 8 s. If each retry shows a fresh panel (not verified, Not
  verified 18), a reader right 90% of the time per arrow solves 99% of spinning runes within 3 tries, an 80% reader
  95%. So the test is a gate against a bad reader; the supervised in-game sessions (H-5, C22) show whether the chosen
